# Payment terms (MSME suppliers)

Under the MSMED Act 2006 a buyer must pay a micro or small enterprise within the agreed period,
and never later than 45 days from acceptance of the goods (s.15). Late payment carries interest at
three times the RBI bank rate, compounded monthly (s.16). The Income-tax Act s.43B(h) also defers
the buyer's deduction for such payments until they are actually paid.

The limit is configurable in `params.json` (`payment_term_days`). Delaying payables is allowed only
up to that limit. See [[treasury-policy]].
